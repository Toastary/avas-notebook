## Commentary

In 2025, [[Research/Library/People/Donald Trump\|Donald Trump]] claimed that his ballroom will be funded and paid for by private investors. This raised concerns on whether [[Research/Library/People/Donald Trump\|Donald Trump]] was lying, or telling the truth.

Today, the ballroom will be paid by the American tax payers. This bill will be led by Sen. [[Research/Library/People/Chuck Grassley\|Chuck Grassley]] (R-[[Research/Library/Geography/Iowa\|Iowa]]), and supported by Sen. [[Research/Library/People/Lindsey Graham\|Lindsey Graham]] (R-S.C.).
