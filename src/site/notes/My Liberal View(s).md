	+ The government has the duty to ensure the well-being of its people

+ Keynesian Economics
	+ [Keynesian Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keynesian_economics) | Wikipedia.
		+ On a macro-level (macroeconomics)
			+ Basically explains that in a market economy it often experiences inefficient macroeconomic outcomes, including recessions when demand is too low and inflation when demand is too high these economic fluctuations can be mitigated by economic policy responses coordinated between a government and their central bank
				+ In the U.S. it would be the [[Research/Library/Geography/United States\|United States]] government and the Federal Reserve
				+ Practicality:
					+ The New Deal (1930s)
					+ World War II (1940s)
					+ The 1960s (Kennedy and Johnson)
					+ The Great Recession (2008)
					+ The COVID-19 Pandemic (2020-2022)
