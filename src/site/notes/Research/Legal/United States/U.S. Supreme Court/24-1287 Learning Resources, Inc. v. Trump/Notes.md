## Relevance

Does the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) authorize the President to impose tariffs? President [[Research/Library/People/Donald Trump\|Donald Trump]] invoked IEEPA to increase tariffs on US imports, we've seen these tariffs gone from 25%-100%. Did the statute grant the authority over tariffs to the [[Research/Library/Goverment/Executive\|Executive]]? Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 grants Congress the authority to impose taxes, tariffs are a tax on imports. This court case reinterpreted the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA), 50 U.S.C. § 1701 doesn't grant the authority to impose tariffs.
