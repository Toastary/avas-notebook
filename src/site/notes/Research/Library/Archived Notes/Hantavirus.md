1. No approved FDA medication(s). Also, no treatment(s), and cure
2. Rarely spreads by human-to-human contact
	+ You can contract it if you are around rodents (rats, and/or mice) it could be possible to contract it if you also breath the particles of urine that is infected by the hantavirus.
	+ Do not allow saliva of a rodent or a person with hantavirus to even reach your skin or your mouth
	+ Having consensual sex will also expose you to hantavirus if the other person contracted it
3. Mortality rate in the U.S.: 35%-38%
4. States that have been exposed to hantavirus (U.S.)
	+ Texas
	+ California
	+ Georgia
5. The spread of it remains relatively low

+ https://www.cdc.gov/hantavirus/about/index.html
	+ Signs and Symptoms
		Early symptoms
		1. Fatigue
		2. Fever
		3. Muscle aches, especially in the large muscle groups like the thighs, hips, back, and sometimes shoulders
	+ About half of all Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome (HPS) patients also experience
		1. Headaches
		2. Dizziness
		3. Chills
		4. Abdominal problems, like nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain
